# Billing & credits

MatOS tracks **stub credits** for AI units, Late posts, Etsy calls, WhatsApp sends, and API hits.

## Important

- **Stripe is not live.** Label in Ops: “Stripe connect — Phase 4b later”.
- No refunds are processed in-app. Ask an Owner to adjust credit grants under **Ops → Billing**.
- Chatbot must **never invent billing refunds** — escalate to a support ticket.

## Viewing usage

Owner and Operator can open **Ops → Usage** and **Ops → Billing** for balances and estimated $.
