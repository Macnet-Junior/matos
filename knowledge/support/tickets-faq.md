# Support tickets

## Opening a ticket

Any logged-in user can create a ticket under **Support**. Statuses: open, pending, solved.

## Priority

Use **high** for blockers (cannot login, publish stuck). Normal for how-to. Low for suggestions.

## Chatbot

The in-app chatbot answers from FAQ markdown. If it cannot help — especially billing/refunds — it will ask you to open a ticket.
