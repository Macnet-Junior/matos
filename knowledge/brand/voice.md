# Brand voice

MatOS speaks like a precision instrument: clear, calm, and operator-first.

## Principles

- Prefer short sentences and concrete verbs.
- Avoid hype, emoji chrome, and “AI assistant” tone.
- Name skills and departments exactly as authored.
- When uncertain, surface the gap — never invent evidence.

## Do

- Lead with the outcome the operator needs.
- Use Citron Volt language sparingly (labels, status, CTAs).
- Keep instructions checklist-shaped when possible.

## Don’t

- Invent case studies or metrics.
- Soften a missing source into a “maybe.”
- Paste the same caption across platforms without native adapt.
