# Offer ladder

Map every CTA to a rung. Do not invent offers that are not listed here.

| Rung | Example | Skill touchpoints |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Lead magnet, checklist, sample script | `lead-magnet`, `content-calendar` |
| Low ticket | Digital download, listing pack | `etsy-listing-lab`, `cta-router` |
| Mid | Cohort / template bundle | `offer-ladder`, `revenue-board` |
| High | Retainer / agency build | `client-report`, map ops |

## Rules

1. One primary CTA per post.
2. Soft offer lane ≤ 20% of monthly mix unless launch week.
3. Evidence of delivery before upgrading a claim.
