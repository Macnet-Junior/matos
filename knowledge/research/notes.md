# Research notes

Working notes for Research Engine skills (`trend-radar`, `competitor-gap`, `voice-miner`, `offer-fit`).

## Capture format

- **Signal** — what showed up (platform, date, niche)
- **Angle** — one-sentence opportunity
- **Fit** — which offer rung it could feed
- **Sources** — links only; no fabricated quotes

## Weekly hygiene

1. Cull angles older than 14 days unless evergreen.
2. Promote winners into `hook-lab` with brand voice attached.
3. Log gaps as Missing on the map — do not paper over them.
